The article explains two tasks. Use these notes from our engineers:

- Export a report as PDF: open the report, then File → Export → PDF. Shortcut: Cmd+Shift+E.
- Turn on automatic backups: Ledgerly → Settings → Backups, then switch on "Back up automatically".
  The backup status also shows as a menu bar icon, and you can add it to Control Center.
- Customers who use Ledgerly on several Macs can sync their reports through iCloud Drive.
- To duplicate a report quickly, press Cmd+D, or right-click the report in the sidebar and choose
  Duplicate.
