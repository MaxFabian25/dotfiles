Write a short how-to for the blog of our British company, Fieldnote Ltd. It explains how customers
set up our app's widget on iPhone and share a field report by video call. Return only the article,
in Markdown.

Notes from our product team:

- The widget works on iPhones running iOS 26 or later.
- Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the Edit button, then Add Widget, and choose Fieldnote.
- To show the widget on the Lock Screen, open Settings → Wallpaper.
- Check the iPhone's battery settings if the widget stops updating: Low Power Mode pauses
  background refresh.
- To discuss a report live, FaceTime your colleague and use SharePlay to show the report.
- Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center and turn on Do Not Disturb during
  site visits.
