7. CONVERSION

7.1 Conversion Right. Subject as hereinafter provided, each Bondholder shall have the right to
convert each Bond of £1,000 principal amount into Ordinary Shares at the Conversion Price, being
£12.50 per Ordinary Share as at the Issue Date, such that the number of Ordinary Shares to be
issued upon conversion of each Bond shall be determined by dividing the principal amount of such
Bond by the Conversion Price in effect on the relevant Conversion Date (the "Conversion Right").

7.2 Conversion Period. The Conversion Right may be exercised only (a) during any calendar quarter
if the Closing Price of the Ordinary Shares on at least 20 of the 30 consecutive Dealing Days
ending on the last Dealing Day of the immediately preceding calendar quarter exceeded 130 per cent.
of the Conversion Price, or (b) at any time following the announcement of an Offer, or (c) at any
time during the period of 60 days ending on the Maturity Date.

7.3 Fractions. No fraction of an Ordinary Share shall be issued upon conversion and no cash
payment shall be made in lieu thereof.

7.4 Maturity. Bonds which have not been converted shall be redeemed at their principal amount on
the Maturity Date, being 1 December 2031.
