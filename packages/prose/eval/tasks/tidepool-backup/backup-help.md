## Back up your Tidepool library

Tidepool keeps a copy of your library on the iPhone, so check the iPhone's free storage before
your first backup. If you own several iPads, you can back up each one separately.

1. Tap on the **Tidepool** icon on the home screen.
2. Go to *Settings* → *Backup* and flip the "back up over cellular" toggle to on.
3. To check a backup's progress, swipe down to open the Control Centre.

On a Mac:

1. Click on the Tidepool menu and select *Settings…*.
2. Hit **Cmd+Opt+B** to start a backup at once, or right click the library in the sidebar and pick
   "Back Up Now".
3. If you want to talk a colleague through a restore, just FaceTime them and share your screen.
