SUMMARY OF THE OFFER

The following summary is qualified in its entirety by reference to, and should be read in
conjunction with, the more detailed information appearing elsewhere herein. Capitalised terms used
but not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed thereto in the Trust Deed.

Fenmoor Rail Holdings plc (the "Issuer" or the "Company") hereby offers 6.25 per cent. Guaranteed
Bonds due 2032 (the "Bonds") in denominations of £100. The Bonds and the guarantee thereof (the
"Guarantee") by Fenmoor Rail Services Limited ("FRSL") will constitute unsecured senior obligations
of the Issuer and FRSL, respectively.

Interest on the Bonds shall be payable semi-annually in arrear on 15 April and 15 October in each
year, the first such payment to be made on 15 April 2027.

Bondholders shall be entitled to require the Issuer to redeem their Bonds at 101 per cent. of their
principal amount together with accrued interest if a Change of Control occurs and, within 120 days
thereafter, the Bonds are downgraded by Fitch to below BBB-, or if the Issuer disposes of assets
with an aggregate value exceeding 25 per cent. of Consolidated Total Assets in any financial year
and does not apply the Net Proceeds thereof towards repayment of debt or reinvestment in the
business within 365 days of such disposal, provided that no Bondholder shall be entitled to
exercise such right if the Issuer has, prior to the relevant event, given notice of redemption of
all of the Bonds pursuant to Condition 7(b).

Condition 4 (Negative Pledge): "So long as any Bond remains outstanding, the Issuer will not, and
will procure that none of its Subsidiaries will, create or permit to subsist any Security Interest
upon the whole or any part of its present or future undertaking, assets or revenues to secure any
Relevant Indebtedness unless the Bonds are secured equally and rateably therewith."

Risk factors: The Issuer's business is subject to risks generally associated with the industries
in which it operates, and any of such risks could have a material adverse effect on the Issuer's
business, financial condition, results of operations and prospects. There can be no assurance that
the Issuer will be able to meet its obligations under the Bonds. Investors should note that the
Bonds are unsecured senior obligations of the Issuer.

In order to apply for Bonds, investors must apply through their stockbroker prior to 5 p.m. on
30 November 2026.
