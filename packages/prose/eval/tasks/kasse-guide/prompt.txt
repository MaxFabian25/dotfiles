Product: Kasse, a point-of-sale web app for small retailers. We sell it to retailers across the EU,
and it falls under the European Accessibility Act, so our documentation must meet EN 301 549.

Facts:

- Kasse runs in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, on desktop and iPad.
- Staff sign in with their staff number and a four-digit PIN.
- A sale: scan or search for items, choose a payment method (card, cash, or MobilePay), then take
  payment. The receipt prints or is emailed.
- Refunds need a manager's PIN.
- End-of-day report: Reports > End of day. It shows takings by payment method and the cash that
  should be in the drawer.
- Accessibility features:
  - Every function works from the keyboard. Shortcuts: F2 new sale, F4 search items, F8 take
    payment, Esc cancels the current step.
  - High-contrast theme: Settings > Display > High contrast.
  - Text size: Settings > Display > Text size, from 100% to 200%.
  - Screen readers: checked with NVDA 2026.2 on Windows 11 and VoiceOver on iPadOS 26.
- Support: support@kasse.example, phone +45 70 20 30 40, weekdays 08:00 to 17:00.
