Write the "Accessibility conformance" section of the Kasse user guide from the facts below. Use

Facts:

- Kasse is a point-of-sale web app sold to retailers across the EU. It falls under the European
  Accessibility Act.
- In October 2026, Nordwind Accessibility ApS audited Kasse against EN 301 549.
- Kasse meets all applicable requirements except two:
  - The receipt preview image has no text alternative.
  - The refund status shows only as red text, with no other cue.
- Both are planned to be fixed in the March 2027 release.
- Accessibility feedback: support@kasse.example.
