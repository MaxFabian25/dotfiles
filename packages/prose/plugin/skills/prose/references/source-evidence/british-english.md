# British English source evidence

## British English

The GOV.UK guide is the selected public authority for UK English and the deliberate *-ise* house
convention. The Collins entries establish the British noun–verb distinctions for *licence* and
*license* and for *practice* and *practise*. The [British English spelling
section](../english-core.md#british-english-spelling) records the selected forms.
Attribute the house convention only to this skill.

The Cambridge grammar entries establish British usage for the house distinctions among *once* and
*after*, *inside* and *outside*, and *onto* and *on to*. This skill selects narrower forms only where
they state the intended relation more directly.
