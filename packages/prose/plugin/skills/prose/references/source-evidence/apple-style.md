# Apple source evidence

## Apple Style Guide

The [Apple style](../apple-style.md) adapter is independently worded from the June 2026 *Apple
Style Guide*. It uses these entries: *key, keys* (p. 120) for hyphenated key combinations and
modifier order; *button* (p. 43), *menus* (p. 139), and *option names* (p. 153) for plain-text
interface names in quotation marks; and *possessives* (p. 163), *product names* (p. 168), *iPhone*
(p. 116), and *trademarks (usage)* (p. 207) for product names. Page numbers refer to the PDF
export of that edition.

The adapter keeps only the forms that unrestricted writers missed in the October 2026 recheck. It
leaves out entries that writers already followed, such as *Control-click* and *choose*. The guide's
US spelling, punctuation inside quotation marks, and US date and unit forms stay outside the
adapter, so house forms govern the surrounding prose.

A British English Mac shows *Control Centre*, while the guide writes *Control Center*. The
interface-language rule in [Interface instructions](../interface-instructions.md) settles such
pairs by the readers' interface. That choice is a house choice.
