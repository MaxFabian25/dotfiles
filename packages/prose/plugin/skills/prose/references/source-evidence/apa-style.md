# APA source evidence

## APA Style

The skill uses Chapters 1, 2, 4–10, and 12 of the manual; Chapter 11 was checked for filings. The
chapters used cover scholarly communication, paper format, grammar, usage, word choice, voice,
bias-free language, mechanics, tables, figures, citations, references, reference examples, and
copyright attribution. The [APA Style
module](../apa-style.md), [APA citations and references](../apa-citations-and-references.md), and
[source-text rules for quotations in relation to text](../source-text.md#quotations-in-relation-to-text)
contain independently worded adaptations, checked against the full text of the manual. The APA
module keeps the 40-word block threshold of Section 8.27 inside APA scope. Source Text provides
general house mechanics without a numeric threshold. Its Chicago forms apply only when an explicit
Chicago quotation scope controls. No extended quotation or reproduction licence is included.

The manual governs. It gives no explicit day and month order for running text, although Section 6.3
assumes month, day, and year; the house date form is therefore a stated departure. Section 7.13
centres numerical table values and permits decimal alignment, so right alignment is the house
departure. No section places a unit that applies to a whole table; the general-note placement
extends Sections 7.12 and 7.14. The manual has no example for filings with the U.S. Securities and
Exchange Commission, earnings calls, investor presentations, or commercial financial databases.
The skill adapts the nearest categories, as Section 9.1 directs, using the report form of Section
10.4, the slide form of Section 10.14, the recorded-webinar form of Section 10.12, and the data set
and database elements of Sections 10.9, 9.30, and 9.34.

## APA Style website

The 2020 manual contains no guidance on generative AI. The APA Style website's generative AI
reference series of 9 September 2025 and its AI reference examples supply the AI forms. The website
otherwise serves as supplemental content that the manual refers to; where it and the manual differ,
the manual governs.

### APA supporting guidance

The University of Notre Dame Mahaffey Business Library guide supports the business-library practice
of citing screens and exports from commercial financial databases with the data set form.
