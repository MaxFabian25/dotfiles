# ASD-STE100 source evidence

## ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

The skill uses selected Part 1 writing rules and exact source designations from Parts 1 and 2. The
[ASD-STE100 module](../asd-ste100.md) contains independently worded adaptations. It does not reproduce
the standard or its dictionary. The home page supplies the classification *controlled natural
language* in the first paragraph under “Welcome to the STEMG website”.
