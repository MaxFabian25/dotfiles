# Multimedia

Apply this branch when a Chicago citation concerns an audio, video, performance, podcast, game,
or other multimedia source.

This module records the cited multimedia source and the version consulted. It does not license
reuse or replace a platform's governing requirements.

## Classify the multimedia source

- Classify the source before gathering its multimedia metadata.
- Select audio recording, film or video recording, live performance, online video, podcast, or
  video game or app.
- Treat an audiobook as an electronic book when the published book edition is the cited source.
- Use an audio-recording type when the performance or recording itself supports the claim.
- Distinguish the original work, its recording or release, the consulted copy, and any excerpt.
- Give an independently released episode, track, or scene its own record when the claim depends on
  it.
- Treat a live performance as a recording only when readers can consult the identified recording.
- Route comments on online media through [Websites, blogs, social media, interviews, and personal
  communications](chicago-web-and-personal-sources.md).

## Record the source type

| Source type | Required source elements | Other source elements and locators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audio recording | Author, performer, conductor, or other primary contributor and role, item title, album or recording title, publisher, and date | Recording and release dates, contributors, acquisition number, medium, version, track, time stamp, duration, and URL |
| Film or video recording | Director or other primary contributor and role, movie, television show, or episode title, publisher or distributor, release date, and version consulted | Series, season, episode, contributors, original date, medium, scene, time stamp, duration, language data, and URL |
| Live performance | Work or description, people responsible for the work or performance, venue, place, and performance date | Production title, programme details, and any separately cited recording |
| Online video | Person, group, or other entity responsible for content, title or description, publisher or other entity responsible for making content available, platform, posting date, and URL | Original source facts, event and recording dates, channel, sponsor, other contributors, version, duration, and time stamp |
| Podcast | Host, series title, episode title or description, publisher, release date, and URL | Season, episode number, producer, other contributors, version, duration, and time stamp |
| Video game or app | Developer or publisher, title, version, release date, and platform or operating system | Build, edition, device, distributor, URL, level, scene, state, or another specific locator |

- Record only fields supplied by the source or supported by verified evidence.
- Mark a date or version as unavailable only after checking the direct source and an authoritative
  catalogue.

## Record responsibility and titles

- Choose the author, performer, or other primary contributor according to the content under
  discussion.
- Preserve each contributor's role. Do not present a performer, composer, director, host, or
  uploader as interchangeable.
- For music, select the composer, performer, conductor, or ensemble according to the cited focus.
- For an untitled item, supply a concise description without making it look like a source title.
- Record each item, track or episode, series or album, and platform in order.
- Do not let an uploader or channel replace the person, group, or entity responsible for the
  original content.
- Record the publisher, label, distributor, network, sponsor, platform, or channel in its actual
  role.
- Verify uncertain recording details against an authoritative catalogue or responsible publisher.

## Distinguish dates, media, and versions

- Label recording, performance, original-release, consulted-release, posting, and update dates by
  function.
- Include both recording and release dates when both identify the consulted source.
- Record the medium when it distinguishes the version consulted or affects the locator.
- Record a streaming service or file format only when it helps identify the consulted copy.
- For a video game or app, record the version, build when needed, release date, and supported
  platform.
- Distinguish an original source from a digitised, streamed, reissued, remastered, or excerpted
  copy.
- Record enough facts about both objects when the consulted copy differs from the original.
- Prefer a copy supplied by the author, performer, publisher, sponsor, repository, or authorised
  distributor.
- Do not treat an unaffiliated upload as evidence of authenticity or permission.
- Apply [Rights, permissions, and credit
  lines](references-and-citations.md#rights-permissions-and-credit-lines) before reproducing or adapting
  protected media.

## Supply a specific locator

- Give the most specific locator available for the cited material.
- Use a time stamp or range, track, scene, episode, chapter, frame, slide, level, or named section as
  applicable.
- Make every locator match the consulted version rather than another release.
- Record the version when different releases have different timings, tracks, scenes, or levels.
- Include total duration only when it helps readers identify or assess the item.
- Test every URL and specific locator against the source record.
- Do not invent a track or scene label for an unindexed work.
- For a live performance, identify the venue, place, and date. Include enough information to
  identify the performance.

## Select the citation form

- Use the selected Chicago citation system after the source record is complete.
- Put recordings in a separate discography or filmography only when the project's citation system
  calls for one.
- Use the same verified source record in a bibliography, discography, filmography, note, or
  reference list.
- Cite a live performance that readers cannot consult as such in prose or a note. Add a list entry
  only when project policy requires it.
- Cite a video game or app in prose or a note unless project policy requires a list entry.
- When a list entry is required, choose its lead element according to the cited responsibility.
- Preserve a stable URL or permitted copy when an online source may change or disappear.
- Apply [Link selection and behaviour](references-and-citations.md#link-selection-and-behaviour) to
  the URL, [Citation placement and style](references-and-citations.md#citation-placement-and-style)
  to the version and date, and [Locators](chicago-source-forms.md#locators) to the locator.

Complete this branch when every cited source meets its medium's metadata, availability, locator,
and citation-form requirements. Report any unresolved required field.
