# Chicago author-date

Apply this branch when the selected Chicago citation system is author-date.

Use the Chicago forms in [Chicago source forms](chicago-source-forms.md). Do not import APA
punctuation, author counts, suffixes, or ordering.

## Author-date reference lists

- Title the list *References* or *Works Cited*.
- Place it at the work's end before any index.
- Give each journal article its own list.
- Give a chapter its own list when chapters have different authors or circulate separately.
- Match every text citation to one entry under the same name and date.
- Write the year without parentheses and follow it with a full stop.
- When no author or editor is listed, begin the entry with the title, then the year.
- Use *and*, not an ampersand, between the final names.
- List authors under the [author count for reference lists](chicago-source-forms.md#citation-forms).
- Alphabetise entries word by word unless governing instructions select letter by letter.
- For identical author sequences, arrange works from oldest to newest.
- Place a single-author entry before multiauthor entries beginning with the same name.
- For a shared first author, alphabetise multiauthor entries by the coauthors' surnames.
- For identical author sequences and years, append lowercase letters to the year without spaces.
- Order those entries alphabetically by title, ignoring initial *A*, *An*, or *The*.
- Use one suffix sequence across author, editor, compiler, and translator roles.
- Do not share a suffix sequence between works with different author order.
- Use *n.d.-a* and *forthcoming-a* forms for undated and forthcoming works.

## Author-date text citations

- Put author and year in parentheses with no punctuation between them.
- When the text names the author, put the year in parentheses immediately after the name.
- Do this even when the name is possessive.
- Use *and*, not an ampersand, for two authors.
- For more than two authors, give the first surname followed by et al.
- Keep et al. in roman type.
- Give page numbers without *p.* or *pp.*
- Separate nonconsecutive locators with commas.
- Label paragraph, section, equation, chapter, note, and other nonpage locators.
- Use *vol.* when citing a whole volume without pages.
- Use a stable heading phrase when a source lacks numbered locators.
- Place a parenthetical citation before the following sentence punctuation.
- After a block quotation, place the citation after the quotation's terminal punctuation.
- Do not add a full stop after that closing parenthesis.
- For repeated same-page references in one paragraph, place one citation after the last reference.
- For changing pages, give a full first citation, then page-only citations.

## Multiple author-date text references

- Separate different sources within one parenthesis with semicolons.
- For multiple works by one author, give the surname once and separate years with commas.
- Use semicolons instead when those works include locators.
- Order grouped sources by the material supported or their relative importance.
- Otherwise use a consistent alphabetical or chronological order.
- Follow a journal-specific order when one is prescribed.
- Separate a citation from an added comment with a semicolon.
- Add initials or a given name when different authors share a surname.
- For same-year et al. collisions, add coauthor surnames until the citation keys differ.
- Alternatively, add a distinctive short title between the author and year.
- Set off that short title with commas.
- When no author or editor is listed, cite a short title containing the first nonarticle word.
- Keep that title to four words or fewer when recognition permits.
- Cite an organisation by abbreviation only when its reference entry begins with that abbreviation.

## Author-date system with notes

- Reserve author-date footnotes and endnotes for substantive commentary.
- Format citations inside those notes exactly like text citations.
- Do not move author-date source citations from the text into notes.
- For permitted notes, apply [Note numbers](chicago-notes-and-bibliography.md#note-numbers) and
  [Footnotes versus endnotes](chicago-notes-and-bibliography.md#footnotes-versus-endnotes).
- Those links do not select the notes and bibliography system.

Complete this branch when text citations and reference entries match under the source-type rules,
grouped references follow the selected ordering and disambiguation rules, and permitted notes
preserve the author-date system.
